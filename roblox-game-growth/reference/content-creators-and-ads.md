# Content creators, curated sorts, ads and creator payouts

Roblox rules change often; accurate at writing time (late 2026). Everything that costs money or Robux is
marked 💲: **draft it, price it and get the owner's explicit approval; never spend on your own.**

## Outside attention: YouTube, TikTok, streamers
What the evidence says:
- Roblox: social sharing, friend invites and YouTube drive organic traffic; external traffic helps
  *retrieval* (consideration) but only RFY users' behaviour drives *ranking* [ROBLOX].
- Dead Rails (Feb-Mar 2025): Roblox's own TikTok/IG/X posted it, then Flamingo, KreekCraft and Thinknoodles
  played it; CCU went from hundreds to 100k+ and then ~600k on a weekend [CASE, maxpowergaming].
- DOORS: random rooms and jump scares made reaction videos; big YouTubers and streamers (KreekCraft,
  Thinknoodles, Flamingo, iShowSpeed, xQc) played it [CASE, press].
- Dress to Impress: outfit clips, "Pose 28" memes and real-world trends spread it on TikTok [CASE, press].
- Grow a Garden: streamer-friendly (rare seeds, events, trading) [CASE, third-party analysis].

Playbook [HEURISTIC]:
- **Make the game clip-able first** (see `social-and-virality.md`): readable reactions, rare events,
  chaos with friends, a "did you see that?" moment within the first minute.
- **Post your own clips natively**: short vertical clips with a hook in the first ~2 s, 1-2 a day while
  testing hooks, cross-posted to Shorts/Reels; caption with the game name so search works; link via a
  **share link** in bio/description to measure retained players (community tutorials, anecdotal).
- **Creators**: list 20-50 creators who already play your genre (small and mid-size first; they reply).
  Send a short kit: one-line pitch, 3 GIFs/clips, the share link, a creator-only code or private server,
  the update date. Ask, don't demand. **Paid deals are 💲** and need disclosure under platform rules.
- **Time it**: send before a weekend and right after an update; give creators early access the day before.
- **Don't**: offer Robux/real-value giveaways to viewers (not allowed without Roblox involvement), ask
  creators to say false things, or pay for views/bots.

## Curated surfaces (free, nomination-based) [ROBLOX]
- **Today's Picks (Home)**: daily editorial picks. Nominate through the survey linked in the
  [Today's Picks doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/todays-picks-home); re-nominate at each
  notable update. Only selected games are notified.
- **Standout Games**: a curated sort for novel, high-quality games
  ([doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/standout-games)). Underrepresented genres (RPG,
  strategy, puzzle, shooter) and originality help; reskins are a hard sell. Recommended: console + mobile
  optimisation, modern avatars (R15, layered clothing, dynamic heads), events, localisation, a trailer, compliant thumbnails.
- Other programs (Jumpstart, Incubator, Spotlights, awards) are listed under Creator programs; check
  eligibility before planning around them.

## Ads Manager 💲 [ROBLOX]
- Access: 13+ with a verified email. Pay by card (18+) or **Robux → ad credits** (permanent, irreversible;
  minimum 1 credit). Group-owned games can use the group balance with the right permission.
- Formats: **Sponsored** (Home tile + search, up to 10 creatives), **Search ads** (up to 10 keywords per
  ad set; bid only on relevant keywords: relevance affects auction), **Immersive ads** (billboards,
  **portals** inside other games), **Rewarded video** (you can *earn* by showing them: natural breaks,
  rewards worth ~3-10 Robux per the doc).
- Objectives: **Plays** (broad distribution, testing), **Earnings** (likely spenders), **Engagement**
  (age-checked highly engaged players who count toward the Kids/Select threshold; higher cost per play).
  Audiences include new users and lapsed users (lapsed only for games with ≥ 20,000 lapsed players).
- Auto-bidding with daily or lifetime budgets; optional auto-reload of credits (keep it **off** unless approved).
- Ads don't feed RFY ranking; they buy players and data. Ad users can make RFY impressions dip (not a penalty) [ROBLOX, FAQ].
- Ad creatives must follow advertising standards; clickbait/spam games are not surfaced; violations can
  suspend the game or account.

**Ad test plan (draft for the owner)** [HEURISTIC]: one objective (Plays), one market/language, 3 creatives
(= your best thumbnails), a small fixed lifetime budget, 3-7 days, compare D1 of ad users with organic
users in Retention ▸ by source; stop rule decided before launch. Only after the growth gate passes.

## Creator payouts that reward external traffic [ROBLOX]
- **Creator Affiliate was deprecated (July 2025)** and replaced by **Creator Rewards** (DevForum 3777628):
  - *Daily engagement*: Robux per active spender who plays ≥ 10 min/day (counted for the first few games they open that day).
  - *Audience expansion*: a revenue share on early purchases by new or long-lapsed users who arrive through a
    direct link or a name search and play ≥ 10 min; eligibility thresholds (e.g. average DAU), a hold period
    and payout setup apply. Read the current terms before promising anything.
  - Prohibited: bots/automation/teleport manipulation for visits or engagement, encouraging alt accounts, impersonation.
- Share Link analytics show which external channel brings these users.
