# Launch sequence and live ops

Roblox rules change often; accurate at writing time (late 2026).

## Launch sequence [ROBLOX facts + HEURISTIC order]
1. **Private playtests** (Friends audience or a test place): 10-30 real players, watch them play, fix the
   first 60 s. Run `roblox-game-qa`.
2. **Store page pack** ready (icon, 3-5 thumbnails, video, title, description, genre, questionnaire, languages).
3. **Public, no push**: RFY tests new games quickly (no 28-day wait). Watch Home Recommendations daily but
   decide weekly. Invite your own circle; they help retrieval, not ranking.
4. **Iterate** on PTR (store page) and bounce/D1 (first session) until at/above benchmark.
5. **Push** after the growth gate: update + event + creators + Today's Picks nomination in the same week,
   timed to land before a **Saturday** (weekly peak) and ideally a holiday or school break.

## Update cadence [CASE]
- Grow a Garden: "pumping out updates every week on a Friday" (Jandel, quoted by GameAnalytics); from ~500 to
  ~100k CCU in two weeks after live updates (GamesBeat interview).
- 99 Nights in the Forest: weekly updates with a ~45-minute **"Update Party"** (a sandbox countdown session)
  before each update; was still near the top after two weeks without updates (DevForum Creator Spotlight).
- Scary Shawarma Kiosk: weekly updates after Roblox staff got involved (e1.ru interview).
- [HEURISTIC] Small teams: pick a sustainable rhythm (weekly small, monthly big) and keep the day fixed so
  players learn it. Each update must give a reason to come back *today* (new item/creature/map, limited event).

## Each update checklist
- [ ] One clear headline feature + changelog line at the top of the description ("🚨 NEW: …").
- [ ] New thumbnail challenger showing the new thing (personalization stays on).
- [ ] Event page (with its own thumbnail) and/or update announcement; notification to opted-in players.
- [ ] Today's Picks re-nomination; creator heads-up with the date.
- [ ] Questionnaire re-answered if content changed (new scary/violent content).
- [ ] Funnel and economy checks 48 h and 7 days after.

## Seasonal calendar [ROBLOX seasonality + HEURISTIC]
- Weekly: Saturday peak, weekday lows. Yearly: summer/back to school, holidays.
- Big beats: Halloween (horror spikes), Christmas/New Year, Easter, Roblox platform events.
- Per market: e.g. Brazil school breaks (July, December-February), Festa Junina (June), Carnaval.
- Ship the event **before** the date (players RSVP and creators need lead time).

## Copycats and trends [CASE + ROBLOX]
- Trends move fast (survive-N-days, grow/garden, anomaly/rule-based horror, kiosk/shift jobs).
  Roblox's advice: add your own spin to the trend in title, images, description and content.
- Expect clones of a hit: Scary Shawarma Kiosk's creator rushed release before Halloween because a copy with
  the same rules appeared. Roblox favours the original among near-identical games, but speed and a distinct
  identity (setting, art, cultural flavour) matter more than complaints.
- Use the DevForum (bug reports, feedback) and, for hiring help, the Talent Hub; never reuse someone else's assets.
