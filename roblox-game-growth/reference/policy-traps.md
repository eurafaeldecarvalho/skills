# Policy traps (what cuts reach, gets games moderated or accounts banned)

Roblox rules change often; accurate at writing time (late 2026). Sources: [Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards),
[Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery), [Social links](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/social-media-links),
[Advertising standards](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/comply-with-advertising-standards), Creator Rewards terms (DevForum 3777628).

## Reduced exposure (no ban, but RFY/search cut) [ROBLOX]
- **Leading with giveaways**: metadata implying monetary rewards ("Robux! Play now!", "FREE UGC").
- **Mismatched metadata**: title/thumbnail promise things the game doesn't have.
- **Non-unique games**: title, visuals or place files closely resembling existing games.
- **Spam**: repeated words in the name, irrelevant or repeated keywords in the description.
- Check the Creator Dashboard banner (updated daily); fix and wait for reclassification after an update.

## Prohibited (moderation / takedown / ban) [ROBLOX]
- Deceptive, sensational, duplicative or misleading content or metadata used to drive discoverability,
  monetization or engagement, including content confusingly similar to other creators'.
- Repeatedly uploading near-identical games; games whose main purpose is to send users off Roblox; spam.
- **Bots/automation** that inflate visits, engagement, referrals or ad impressions/teleports (ads: Robux
  clawback or suspension); encouraging **alt accounts**; impersonation; false reporting of competitors.
- **Contests/giveaways with Robux or real-world-value prizes** without Roblox involvement; off-platform
  promotions bundled with in-game rewards need an agreement with Roblox.
- **Off-platform links or handles inside the game** (text, signs, decals, thumbnails, video). Only the
  details-page **social links** are allowed: creator age-verified 16+, up to 3 links, visible to age-checked 16+.
  In-game wording allowed: "Check out our social media links on our game's page".
- In-experience advertising outside Roblox's systems (third-party ad calls), unregistered ad integrations,
  personalised ads to under-18s (Advertising integrations policy, 2026).
- Copyrighted music, characters or logos (also in thumbnails and video); videos with lyrics/voice-over are rejected.
- Wrong maturity answers (e.g. hiding gore to reach younger players).

## Self-inflicted growth killers [HEURISTIC]
- Renaming weekly; swapping all thumbnails daily; judging changes in 24 h.
- Paying for traffic before D1 holds; auto-reload on ad credits without a cap.
- A multiplayer-only game that strands solo players in an empty lobby.
- Debug overlays/prints left on in production (they look broken in creators' videos).
- Ignoring the Kids/Select requirements while targeting kids (the game is invisible to them).
