# Templates (fill in, keep honest)

## 1. Description
```
[HOOK ≤160 chars] <Name> is a <genre/mode> where you <core action> — <most unique twist>. <Solo/2-4 players/co-op>.

[RECENT UPDATE — 1-2 lines, optional]
🚨 NEW (<date or update name>): <headline feature>. <second thing>.

[MAIN — 3-6 short lines/bullets, natural keywords]
🎯 <goal of a session in one line>
👥 <social hook: play with friends, roles, trading, co-op>
🔓 <progression: unlocks, collections, endings, ranks>
🎃/⭐ <events, rare spawns, limited content>
🌎 <languages / devices: phone, PC, console>

[EXTRA — optional]
Join the <group name> community for update news. Made by <studio>.
```
Checklist: first sentence says genre + what you do; no controls list; no hashtags; no giveaways; every
feature exists in the build; ≈ 1,000 characters max; hand-written version for the top 2-3 languages.

