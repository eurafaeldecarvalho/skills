## 5. Pre-submit checks

- [ ] Lengths: name ≤30, short ≤80, full ≤4,000.
- [ ] No emoji, emoticons, repeated symbols or ALL CAPS in name, icon, developer name.
- [ ] No "best", "#1", "top", "popular", "app of the year", award icons, "Editor's Choice", "new".
- [ ] No "free", "% off", "sale", "limited time" in name, icon, developer name, short description.
- [ ] No keyword lists / word blocks / irrelevant terms; head term ~3-5× in the full description at most.
- [ ] No unattributed testimonials, no competitor comparison, no third-party brands without permission.
- [ ] Icon has no fake notification dot / download arrow.
- [ ] Screenshots show the real app; ≥4 at ≥1080 px; alt text set.
- [ ] Translations added for each target language (CSLs are not auto-translated).
- [ ] Privacy policy URL, ads declaration, data safety, content rating, reviewer login done.
